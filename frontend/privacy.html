Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 1, 2026

At GITAcademy, your privacy matters deeply to us. This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it. We are committed to full transparency and compliance with applicable data protection laws.
Section 01

What Data We Collect

Data TypeExamplesHow Collected
Account DataName, email, password (hashed)Registration form
Profile DataPhoto, bio, location, social linksProfile editor
Payment DataLast 4 digits, billing countryPayment processor (Stripe)
Usage DataCourses viewed, progress, notesAutomatically via platform
Device DataBrowser, OS, IP addressAutomatically via cookies
💳 We never store your full card number. Payments are processed by Stripe and PayPal, which are PCI-DSS compliant.
Section 02

How We Use Your Data

  • To provide, operate and improve the LearnHub platform
  • To process payments and send purchase receipts
  • To personalise course recommendations based on your interests
  • To send service updates and security alerts
  • To send marketing emails (you can unsubscribe at any time)
  • To detect and prevent fraud and security threats
Section 03

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with: service providers (Stripe, AWS, SendGrid); instructors (aggregated, anonymised data only); and when required by law or court order.

Section 04

Cookies & Tracking

We use essential, analytics, preference and marketing cookies. You can control cookie preferences in your browser settings or via our Cookie Settings panel.

Section 05

Your Rights

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Portability: Request your data in a machine-readable format
  • Objection: Object to processing for marketing purposes

To exercise any of these rights, email support@gitacademy.example. We will respond within 30 days.

Section 06

Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including 256-bit SSL encryption, hashed passwords (bcrypt), regular security audits, and strict access controls.

Section 07

Children's Privacy

GITAcademy is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has registered without parental consent, contact us at support@gitacademy.example and we will delete the account.

Section 08

Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will delete or anonymise your personal data within 90 days, except where required by law.

Section 09

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify you of material changes via email or a prominent in-app notice at least 14 days before the changes take effect.
