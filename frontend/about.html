Our Story

Learning is a
human right.

GITAcademy was built on one belief: that world-class education should be accessible to everyone, everywhere — regardless of where you were born or how much money you have.

Our Mission

We exist to
unlock potential.

Founded in 2020 by a team of educators and engineers across Africa and beyond, GITAcademy started with a simple question: why do the best courses only reach people who already have access to the best universities?

Today we serve 2 million learners in 150+ countries, partnering with the world's best instructors to deliver practical, career-changing skills at prices that make sense everywhere in the world.
